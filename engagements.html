Engagements

Every engagement is scoped to a specific upcoming event, with a real date — an audit, an exam, a customer review, a diligence process. That's what makes the findings actionable instead of theoretical.

01

Diagnostic

4–6 weeks. You have a specific audit, exam, review, or diligence event on the calendar and need to know where the chain breaks before it happens. This is a fixed-fee, fixed-scope operational design assessment anchored to that event — SOC 2 Type II, SOX readiness, ISO 27001 or HITRUST, a state regulatory examination, a named strategic-account customer security review, or fundraise diligence. You receive a defensible written assessment with prioritized remediation, and a proposal for follow-on work if any is needed.

Most companies start here. It's the entry point that scopes everything else.

02

Design Engagement

6–12 weeks. A Diagnostic identified structural design defects — broken links in the chain, or missing risk translations — that need to be redesigned rather than patched. This engagement redesigns the specific broken links and bridges the Diagnostic surfaced. You receive a redesigned operational chain, built with your internal owners so it holds together after the engagement ends.

For: Companies that have completed a Diagnostic (or have an equivalent internal read) and have a named internal counterpart who will own the chain afterward.

03

Focused Build

4–10 weeks. The chain is already designed correctly, but execution capacity is the constraint — you know what needs to be built, you just don't have the hands to build it. This engagement executes the specific remediations identified in a Diagnostic. You receive the remediations built and in place, ready to run under your ownership.

For: Companies where the design work is done and the gap is capacity, not clarity.

Design notes & Google Sites tradeoffs

What you're looking at

This is the v3 copy revision. Engagement names and durations now match the Goal-Anchored Operations one-pager exactly: Diagnostic (4–6 weeks), Design Engagement (6–12 weeks), Focused Build (4–10 weeks) — replacing the earlier five-format portfolio (Briefing / Diagnostic / Design Engagement / Steady State / Build). Visual design, layout, and card structure are unchanged.

This is a faithful static mockup of the approved design system. Type sizes, spacing, colors are exact. Use it to judge the aesthetic; the live build will happen in Google Sites and may diverge in the ways noted below.

Placeholders: bryan@southpillarllc.com and the github.com/southpillar/south-pillar canonical link in the footer are both placeholders pending final domain and email decisions.

Google Sites parity check

  • FULL Custom hex palette (5 colors) — Google Sites custom theme accepts any hex.
  • FULL Source Serif 4 + Inter — both available via "More fonts" in the theme editor.
  • FULL 680px prose width — achieved by leaving outer grid columns empty.
  • PARTIAL Bronze underlines on inline links — Sites supports custom link colors but with less precise control over offset/thickness. Expect ~80% fidelity.
  • PARTIAL Eyebrow letter-spacing — Sites doesn't expose letter-spacing directly; close using its "small heading" style.
  • NOT IN SITES Sticky header with subtle scroll behavior — Sites uses a fixed nav style. Acceptable compromise.
  • NOT IN SITES This annotation drawer — mockup-only.

Page-specific notes

  • Engagements are numbered 01–03 in eyebrows — there are exactly three.
  • No prices anywhere on the page. The Diagnostic is the qualification gate.

What's intentionally absent

  • No icons, no decorative graphics, no stock imagery.
  • No social proof, no testimonials, no client logos.
  • No pricing.
  • No form. Contact is mailto only.
  • No new jargon beyond what's in the methodology one-pager.
