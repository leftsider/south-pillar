Bryan Collick is currently a Director of Operations at Braze, where he built operational governance before and after IPO. He manages a team responsible for capitalization processes (~$5M annually) with clean audit controls, R&D tax credit processes (~$6M annually), tech vendor governance frameworks, PMO operations, org-wide OKR governance, Looker-based reporting and other operational initiatives. He coordinated regulatory examination response at LendUp during active state and federal regulatory examinations, and self-certification work at Babylon Health. The through-line across his career is not the regulation in each case; it's the underlying operational layer that had to be designed for any of these companies to produce defensible outcomes. South Pillar is the practice he founded to do that work for other companies, through contracted engagement rather than as full-time staff.

South Pillar exists as a practice because the work has a shape. The operational layer underneath audit, compliance, finance, and risk fails in patterned ways at growth-stage companies: policies inherited from templates, controls accreted from audit findings, processes that diverge from what's written, continuous review that quietly disappears the first time a quarter goes sideways. The patterns are visible from the inside, and they are fixable — but not by adding tooling or headcount on top of a broken chain. The method South Pillar uses is called Goal-Anchored Operations, and it is documented in full on the methodology page.

South Pillar is based in Texas. Engagements are remote-first, with on-site working sessions where the work calls for them.

Google Sites parity check

  • FULL Custom hex palette (5 colors) — Google Sites custom theme accepts any hex.
  • FULL Source Serif 4 + Inter — both available via "More fonts" in the theme editor.
  • FULL Five-link chain — built as an embedded HTML/CSS block; identical to this preview.
  • FULL 680px prose width — achieved by leaving outer grid columns empty.
  • PARTIAL Bronze underlines on inline links — Sites supports custom link colors but with less precise control over offset/thickness. Expect ~80% fidelity.
  • PARTIAL Eyebrow letter-spacing — Sites doesn't expose letter-spacing directly; close using its "small heading" style.
  • PARTIAL 64px bronze rule — Sites' divider can't be resized to exactly 64px; we'll use a 64×1 PNG colored bronze. Visually identical.
  • NOT IN SITES Sticky header with subtle scroll behavior — Sites uses a fixed nav style. Acceptable compromise.
  • NOT IN SITES This annotation drawer — mockup-only.
