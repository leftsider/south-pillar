Bryan Collick built operational governance at Braze through and after IPO — a vendor governance framework from scratch, governance over a material book of capitalized R&D under clean-audit controls, M&A integration of operational functions, and Looker-based reporting that the board, finance, and operating teams use. Earlier, he coordinated regulatory examination response at LendUp during an active state regulatory examination, and self-certification work at Babylon Health. The through-line across these is not the regulation in each case — it is the underlying operational layer that had to be designed for any of them to produce defensible outcomes. South Pillar is the practice he founded to do that work for other companies, on a design engagement rather than as full-time staff.

The practice exists because the work has a shape. The operational layer underneath audit, compliance, finance, and risk fails in patterned ways at growth-stage companies: policies inherited from templates, controls accreted from audit findings, processes that diverge from what's written, continuous review that quietly disappears the first time a quarter goes sideways. The patterns are visible from the inside, and they are fixable — but not by adding tooling or headcount on top of a broken chain. The method South Pillar uses is called Goal-Anchored Operations, and it is documented in full on the methodology page.

South Pillar is based in Texas. Engagements are remote-first, with on-site working sessions where the work calls for them.

Google Sites parity check

  • FULL Custom hex palette (5 colors) — Google Sites custom theme accepts any hex.
  • FULL Source Serif 4 + Inter — both available via "More fonts" in the theme editor.
  • FULL Five-link chain — built as an embedded HTML/CSS block; identical to this preview.
  • FULL 680px prose width — achieved by leaving outer grid columns empty.
  • PARTIAL Bronze underlines on inline links — Sites supports custom link colors but with less precise control over offset/thickness. Expect ~80% fidelity.
  • PARTIAL Eyebrow letter-spacing — Sites doesn't expose letter-spacing directly; close using its "small heading" style.
  • PARTIAL 64px bronze rule — Sites' divider can't be resized to exactly 64px; we'll use a 64×1 PNG colored bronze. Visually identical.
  • NOT IN SITES Sticky header with subtle scroll behavior — Sites uses a fixed nav style. Acceptable compromise.
  • NOT IN SITES This annotation drawer — mockup-only.
