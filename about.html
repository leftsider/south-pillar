South Pillar applies Goal-Anchored Operations to help growing companies build operational infrastructure that produces trust as a byproduct of how they run.

Bryan built financial compliance and vendor governance programs for product and engineering at Braze post-IPO: a technology vendor framework from scratch, ~$11M of capitalized engineering work and R&D tax credits managed under clean-audit controls, M&A integration, and Looker-based reporting used by the board, finance, and audit teams. Before Braze, he coordinated regulatory examination response at LendUp during an active CFPB and California state examination, and led self-certification work at Babylon Health.

The practice comes from having run these programs inside companies, under real examination pressure — not from advisory theory. The method he uses is called Goal-Anchored Operations, documented in full on the methodology page.

Google Sites parity check

  • FULL Custom hex palette (5 colors) — Google Sites custom theme accepts any hex.
  • FULL Source Serif 4 + Inter — both available via "More fonts" in the theme editor.
  • FULL 680px prose width — achieved by leaving outer grid columns empty.
  • PARTIAL Bronze underlines on inline links — Sites supports custom link colors but with less precise control over offset/thickness. Expect ~80% fidelity.
  • NOT IN SITES Sticky header with subtle scroll behavior — Sites uses a fixed nav style. Acceptable compromise.
  • NOT IN SITES This annotation drawer — mockup-only.
